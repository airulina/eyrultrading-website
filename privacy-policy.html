Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EYRUL TRADING handles information when you use EPOS LIVE. EPOS LIVE is a point-of-sale application for business owners and their authorised staff.

Information handled by EPOS LIVE

Depending on how you use the app, EPOS LIVE may handle account and sign-in information, business and branch details, staff account details, product and stock information, sales and transaction records, and information you enter while using the app. The app may also process technical information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

How information is used

Information is used to create and authenticate accounts, provide point-of-sale and business management features, manage branches and staff access, record and display business activity, maintain service security, troubleshoot problems and respond to support requests.

Storage and service providers

Information required to provide EPOS LIVE may be stored and processed on the service's systems, including server infrastructure. EYRUL TRADING may use third-party providers for services such as hosting, authentication or data storage where required to operate the app. Those providers process information only as needed to provide their services to us.

Sharing of information

EYRUL TRADING does not sell your personal information. Information may be disclosed to service providers supporting EPOS LIVE, when required by law, or when necessary to protect the service, its users or the rights and safety of others.

Account deletion and 30-day recovery period

You can request account deletion from within EPOS LIVE after confirming the deletion action. If you cannot access the app, you can start a request through our account deletion page.

After a deletion request is confirmed, the account enters a 30-day recovery period. During this period, the account and associated data are retained temporarily. If you sign in or register again using the same EPOS LIVE account within 30 days, the deletion request is cancelled and the previous data is restored.

If the account is not recovered within 30 days, the account and its associated data are permanently deleted from the EPOS LIVE service. This includes associated business, branch, staff, sales and transaction data. Data that has been permanently deleted cannot be restored.

Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information handled by EPOS LIVE. However, no internet-based service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children

EPOS LIVE is a business tool and is not designed for children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when EPOS LIVE or our data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions or help with account deletion, contact EYRUL TRADING at airulbisnes2024@gmail.com.
