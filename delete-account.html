EPOS LIVE SUPPORT

Request account deletion

EPOS LIVE users can request deletion of their account and associated personal data by emailing our support team.

How to submit a request

  1. Send an email to airulbisnes2024@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line EPOS LIVE Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the account email or user ID linked to EPOS LIVE and enough information for us to verify that you own the account. Do not send your password.
  4. We may contact you to verify the request before processing it.

What happens next?

After verification, we will process the deletion request and delete or anonymise associated personal data, except information we are legally required or permitted to retain. If any data must be retained, we will explain the applicable reason and retention period where possible.

Uninstalling the app

Uninstalling EPOS LIVE from your device does not by itself submit an account deletion request. Please use the email process above.

For help, contact airulbisnes2024@gmail.com.

Operational requirement: the support team must monitor this mailbox and actually complete verified deletion requests. Confirm the real deletion workflow and retention rules before submitting this URL to Google Play Console.
