EPOS LIVE SUPPORT

Account deletion

You can start an account deletion request from the EPOS LIVE app. You can also contact EYRUL TRADING using the email address below if you no longer have access to the app.

Request deletion outside the app

  1. Email airulbisnes2024@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line EPOS LIVE Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address or user ID linked to your EPOS LIVE account so we can identify and verify the account.
  4. Do not include your password or other login credentials.

30-day recovery period

After you confirm an account deletion request in EPOS LIVE, the account enters a 30-day recovery period. During this period, the account and its associated data are retained temporarily so that you can recover them.

If you sign in or register again using the same EPOS LIVE account within those 30 days, the deletion request is cancelled and the previous account data is restored.

Permanent deletion after 30 days

If the account is not recovered within 30 days, the account and its associated data are permanently deleted from the EPOS LIVE service. This includes the account's associated business, branch, staff, sales and transaction data. Once permanent deletion has taken place, the data cannot be restored.

Important

Uninstalling EPOS LIVE does not submit an account deletion request. If you cannot access the app, use the email method above. We may ask for information needed to verify that you own the account. Never send your password.

For help with an account deletion request, contact airulbisnes2024@gmail.com.
